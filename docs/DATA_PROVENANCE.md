state before Phase 1.

present in the repository:

- source URL or Kaggle record;
- dataset version and download date;
- licence and terms of use;
- observed, synthetic, simulated, or augmented status;
- collection or generation method;

- geography represented by the region labels;
- observation dates or time coverage;

as real-world observational evidence or used to make agronomic, causal,
